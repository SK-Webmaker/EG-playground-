# Cold call pack: Kairo + websites (6 Nov 2026)

32 owner-run service businesses in Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane/Logan and Adelaide.
Every one has **no website** (or only Instagram / Facebook / a free page), real reviews, and a phone number that's usually the owner's mobile.
Researched from Google Maps listings and their newest Google reviews (activity, owner replies, names customers mention, complaints).

- **Tier A:** activity verified (reviews in the last 30-60 days) + owner is clearly hands-on. Call these first.
- **Tier B:** active but slower, or a bigger shop. Call second.
- **Tier C:** solid review count but recent activity not checked (ran out of research credits). 20-second Instagram/Google check before dialling.

`04xx` numbers are almost always the owner's own mobile. Landlines `(0x)` usually get the front desk, so ask for the owner by name.

---

## 1. Before you dial (60 seconds per lead)
1. Open their Google listing (search the name + suburb). Read the newest 2-3 reviews out loud once. You'll quote one.
2. Note the **hook** in the table below. It's the one sentence that proves you looked.
3. Check the time zone. Brisbane is 1 hour behind Melbourne/Sydney in summer, Adelaide 30 min behind.
4. Have open: kairobookings.com (to describe screens), your notes, this sheet.

**Best times (their local time)**
| Business | Call | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Barbers, hair, nails, brows | Tue-Thu 9:30-11:00am, 2:00-3:30pm | Saturdays, 3:30-6pm after-school rush |
| Mechanics, plumbers | 12:00-1:30pm (lunch), or after 4pm | First thing (on jobs) |
| Tattoo studios | 11:30am-1:30pm (they open late) | Evenings mid-session |

**Rules:** Mon-Fri 9am-8pm, Sat 9am-5pm, never Sundays or public holidays. Don't block your caller ID. Say your name, Kairo, and why you're calling up front. If someone says "don't call again", note it and never call again. Check any mobile against the Do Not Call Register (donotcall.gov.au) before calling. These are published business numbers, but sole traders' mobiles can be on it.

## 3. Kairo script (booking)

Keep it under 5 minutes. One goal: **book a 10-minute demo or get them signed up on the call.**

**Opener (first 10 seconds)**
> "Hi, is that [Name]? ... It's Sachi from Kairo, I'll be really quick. I was just looking at your Google reviews. [HOOK]. Have you got 30 seconds, or are you mid-client?"

If mid-client: *"No stress, when's better, 2 o'clock or tomorrow morning?"* Book the callback and hang up. Never pitch to someone holding scissors.

**Find the pain (ask, then shut up)**
1. "How do people book with you at the moment?" (They'll say: Instagram DMs, phone, text, walk-ins.)
2. "What happens when you're with a client and someone rings or messages to book?"
3. "Roughly how many no-shows or last-minute cancels do you get in a week?"
4. "Are you paying for any booking app right now?" (If Fresha/Timely/Square: "What does that cost you a month?")

**Show the fix (one line per pain they mentioned, skip the rest)**
- DMs/phone: *"Kairo gives you your own booking link, with your name on it, that you drop in your Instagram bio. People book themselves at 11pm and your phone just pings."*
- No-shows: *"You can take a deposit when they book. Most people with deposits stop getting no-shows almost overnight."*
- Paying monthly: *"It's $410 once. No monthly fee, no commission, ever. If you're paying $50 to $100 a month now, it's paid for itself in a few months."*
- Reminders: *"It texts or emails them a reminder before the appointment automatically."*

**The money line (adapt the number)**
> "If you get even one no-show a week at $40, that's two grand a year walking out the door. Kairo's $410, once."

**Risk reversal**
> "We set it up with you and bring your client list across. And if it's not right within 14 days, you get the full $410 back, no questions."

**Close (always two options)**
> "Easiest thing: I'll set up a demo booking page with your actual services so you can click through it like a client. Want me to send it to this number tonight, or would tomorrow morning suit?"

If they're keen now:
> "Want to just get it going? It takes about 10 minutes. I'll text you the link now and stay on the line while you sign up."

Sign-up link to text: **start.kairobookings.com/start?from=call**

## 4. Website script

**Opener**
> "Hi, is that [Name]? It's Sachi from Kairo, quick one. I was looking at your Google listing: [HOOK]. Have you got 30 seconds?"

**Find the pain**
1. "Where do most of your new customers find you at the moment?"
2. "When someone Googles '[mechanic/plumber/tattoo] in [suburb]' and finds you next to a shop with a proper website, which one do you reckon they call?"
3. "Have you ever thought about a website, or has it just never been worth the hassle?"

**The pitch**
> "All those [167] reviews are doing the hard work already. A simple site just turns them into calls: your work, your prices, your reviews, and a big Call button. People searching at night see it and ring you in the morning."

**Close: free mockup (the offer from our emails)**
> "Here's what I'll do. I'll mock up your homepage for free, with your name, your reviews and your [jobs/tattoos/fades]. I'll text you a screenshot tomorrow. If you like it, we talk. If not, no worries, you've lost nothing. What's the best mobile to send it to?"

**Price, if asked:** *[your website price: decide this before you call]*. Say it once, confidently, then stop talking.

## 5. Objections (short answers)

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "Not interested." | "Totally fair. Can I ask, is that because bookings are already sorted, or just bad timing?" (Their answer tells you whether to leave it or call back.) |
| "Just send me an email." | "Happy to. What's the best address? And so I send the right thing: is it the no-shows or the DMs that bug you more?" (Get a real answer, then email the demo/mockup offer.) |
| "My clients like DMing me." | "They can keep DMing. You just reply with your link and they pick the time. You stop doing the back-and-forth." |
| "I use Fresha / Timely / Square." | "Nice, so you know the value. What's it costing you a month? Kairo's $410 once, then nothing, and you keep your own client list." (For Timely/Square users: pitch the **website** instead.) |
| "Too expensive." | "Compared to what you're paying now, or compared to one no-show a week? It's paid for itself by month five." |
| "I'm too busy to set it up." | "That's exactly why we do it with you. About 10 minutes, and we bring your clients across." |
| "I'm not the owner." | "No worries, who is, and when are they usually in? I'll call back then." (Get the first name.) |
| "We tried a booking app and hated it." | "What went wrong?" (Listen. Kairo's differences are no monthly fee, your own link not a marketplace, and a 14-day full refund.) |
| "I already have Instagram." (website) | "Instagram's great for people who already follow you. Google is for people who've never heard of you. That's the bit a site catches." |
| "Call me later." | "Sure, Tuesday morning or Wednesday afternoon?" (Always lock a time.) |

## 6. Voicemail (under 20 seconds)
> "Hi [Name], it's Sachi from Kairo. I was looking at your Google reviews, [HOOK, one line]. I've got a quick idea for [your bookings / getting more of those people to call you]. I'll try you again [Tuesday morning]. Or call me back on [your number]. Cheers."

Call each lead **up to 3 times** across different days and times, then stop.

## 7. After the call
**If they said yes or asked for info**, text within 5 minutes (only after they've agreed to it on the call):
> "Hi [Name], Sachi from Kairo, thanks for the chat! As promised: [demo link / I'll send your mockup by tomorrow 5pm]. Any questions just text me. — Sachi"

**Log every call** in `call-list.csv`: `status` (no answer / voicemail / callback / not interested / demo booked / sold / do not call), `callback_at`, and `notes`.

Daily target: **25 dials → ~8 conversations → 2 demos.** The demo or free mockup closes the deal, not the call.
