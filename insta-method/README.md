# Insta Method

Instagram outreach for Cairo (booking software + web dev), beauty industry first.

- `leads.csv`: 30 solo beauty operators in Melbourne/VIC and Sydney who take bookings by DM (no booking system, no real website). Ranked by activity and size.
- `profile-mockup.png` / `.html`: recommended layout for @cairo.bookings.

## How the list was built
Public web-search snippets of instagram.com pages (Nov 2026) filtered for:
"DM to book" in the bio or captions, a solo owner-operator, a Melbourne/VIC or Sydney location, and visible activity in 2026.
Instagram itself blocks automated reads, so follower counts come from search snippets and may be a few weeks old.

## Before you DM, check each one in 10 seconds
1. Last post within ~14 days (skip if not)
2. No booking link in the bio (if they already use Fresha/Square/Timely, pitch the website, not booking)
3. They reply to comments (the owner is on the account)
