1Name field = search keywords

Instagram search reads the name line, not just the handle. "Cairo | Booking & Websites for Salons" shows you in searches for "booking" and "salon websites". Keep the handle cairo.bookings.

2Bio: who it's for → pain → proof → action

Lead with the pain they feel every day ("bookings in your DMs"). Name both offers (booking + website). Name the niches so every lash tech or barber sees their own trade. End on one action.

3Use your own booking system on your profile

Make the action button "Book a demo" through Cairo itself. The prospect sees the product working before you pitch it. That's your strongest proof.

4Pin 3 posts that do the selling

  • Pain reel: "Still booking by DM?", screen recording of a messy DM vs. a 30-second booking
  • Hair by Oshi case study: her old setup → her new site and booking page
  • What you get + price: one clear offer card

5Highlights are your website until you have one

Demo · Oshi (case study) · Pricing · How it works · Websites · Reviews. A stylist you DM will tap these before replying.

Link in bio

Point it to one page with: live demo booking page, website examples, and a "book a call" button. Don't use a link tree full of options. Swap in your real domain if it's not the one shown.
