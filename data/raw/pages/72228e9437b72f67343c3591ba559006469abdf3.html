Welcome to
Carlisle Street

A premier food and shopping destination in Melbourne’s south east with over 150 traders. With a thriving village feel, Carlisle Street in the heart of Balaclava is bustling with outstanding cafes, restaurants, shops and services.

Father's Day Competition

Celebrate Dad in style with one of five $100 Carlisle Street vouchers. The perfect gift for dining, shopping, pampering, or just enjoying the best of our local traders.

Gourmet Food

Carlisle Street is bursting at the seams with a plethora of choices to delight and satisfy even the most foodie. Whether it’s the abundance of Jewish bakeries proffering bagels and cakes, or beer gardens brimming with tacos, there’s a global microcosm swirling around Balaclava’s bouncing epicentre. From sushi rolls on the run to sit-down Turkish feasts or fresh fish and chips for a Friday night feed at home, there really is something for everyone. Whether it’s coffee and pastries or cocktails and wine or pub fare and everything in between.

Explore

Explore the fantastic mix of retail stores dotted along Carlisle Street for gifts, beauty, clothes and more. Plus, Carlisle Street has a good blend of professional and health services including optometrists, dental, medical and pharmacies as well as legal, real estate and financial services.
