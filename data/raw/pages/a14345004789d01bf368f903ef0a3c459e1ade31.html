Residential Floor Cleaning

Howards Cleaning Service is a local professional floor cleaning company. We help keep your home clean and fresh! With everyday uses of upholstery, walking on floors with our shoes on, pets and kids, it can be a little much. Not to mention holidays and regular entertainment. We want to make sure your house is always looking it’s best!

Your home is probably the most important investment made for your family. Not only is it your family’s shelter, but it also serves as their comfortable safe place. Keeping it a happy, healthy home becomes much easier with a residential cleaning service specializing in carpets, area rugs, tile & grout, wood, vinyl, and upholstery.

Every day, your family and guests bring in contaminants from outdoors: dust, dirt, automotive oils and fluids, you name it, they track it in. When children and pets are involved, there is no doubt that mud, allergens, hair and pet dander will infiltrate your upholstered furniture. It will collect under the cushions and deep in the pile of your carpets, and it will easily become embedded in your wood and vinyl flooring making it impossible to properly clean.

Then there are always accidents, spills, and subsequent stains. Experts at residential cleaning know the proper methods to remove dirt, stains, and odors, and clean and sanitize the floors and common areas of your residence, helping you maintain the healthiest atmosphere possible for your family and friends.
