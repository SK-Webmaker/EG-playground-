About Us

 Located in the heart of Middletown, NY, we’re more than just a barbershop; we’re a place where clients can unwind, connect, and leave feeling their absolute best. From the moment you walk through the door, you’ll be welcomed with a warm atmosphere, clean setup, and barbers who take pride in every detail.


Our barbers are experienced professionals who specialize in everything from classic cuts to modern fades, straight razor shaves, beard sculpting, and hot towel treatments. Whether you’re coming in for a routine clean-up or a full grooming session, we treat each visit like it matters—because it does. We know that the right cut can boost your confidence, and that’s exactly what we aim to deliver every time.


At Close Shave, we value consistency, quality, and community. We’ve earned the trust of locals by delivering sharp results and treating every client like family. Come in for the cut, stay for the conversation, and leave with a look that turns heads. Your style starts here.
